# Catholic Study Bible — the Study Bible with the complete Greek deuterocanon

A free, word-by-word **Catholic study Bible**: the complete 66-book Study Bible, plus the **complete deuterocanon in Greek** from Swete's public-domain Septuagint — **525,463 words** in all, every word carrying its translation in the verse, a definition, and synonyms.

**Live:** https://jesuslovestyler.github.io/Catholic-Study-Bible/
**Home:** https://jesuslovestyler.com/

## What's inside

- **All 66 books** of the Study Bible exactly as they stand there — 445,671 words in Greek and Hebrew
- **The complete deuterocanon in Greek (82,837 words)** — Tobit, Judith, Greek Esther, Wisdom, Sirach, Baruch with the Letter of Jeremiah as chapter 6, the Greek chapters of Daniel (the Song of the Three, Susanna, Bel), and 1–2 Maccabees
- **Every original word tappable** — its translation in its verse, its definition, and its synonyms and related words
- **Strong's numbers** wherever public-domain sources honestly give one; words with no Strong's number use the supplement lexicon from the abridged Liddell–Scott (1889), honestly marked as unnumbered — nothing is linked that can't be linked truthfully

## The sources

All public domain. Greek New Testament: the Robinson–Pierpont Byzantine Greek Textform 2018, with Strong's Greek Dictionary (1890) and Thayer's lexicon (1889). Hebrew: the pointed Westminster Leningrad Codex, with tagging from BDB (1906) and Strong's. The deuterocanon's Greek: Swete's edition of the Septuagint, with the supplement lexicon abridged from Liddell–Scott (1889). No Creative Commons data is used anywhere in this app.

## The audit

The deuterocanon's word-by-word tagging is being audited to the same standard as the original Study Bible's full-Bible audit — five stages, each published and verified on the live page as it completes. The audit is **in progress**, not finished; this README will say so until the last stage lands.

## The files

`index.html` is the app shell. `assets/` holds the per-book data files, the Greek and Hebrew tag files, and the three lexicons (Greek, Hebrew, and the Septuagint supplement). Word data is plain JavaScript — take it and build.
